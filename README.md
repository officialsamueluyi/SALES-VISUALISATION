# 📊 2019 Annual Sales Analysis – Power BI

## 👋 Overview
This project is a **Power BI dashboard** created to analyze **sales performance in 2019**.  
It highlights key revenue metrics, sales trends, product performance, and geographic distribution to support basic business decision-making.

The project demonstrates foundational skills in **data analysis, visualization, and storytelling** using Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand revenue distribution across cities
- Track monthly sales trends

---

## 📌 Key Metrics
- **Total Revenue:** 34.47M  
- **Number of Sales:** 186K  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
- **Revenue by Product:** Shows products contributing most to total revenue  
- **Revenue by City:** Compares sales performance across locations  
- **Revenue by Month:** Highlights monthly trends and patterns  
- **Sales Table:** Displays detailed sales quantities by product  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Used
- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query** (data cleaning & transformation)
- **DAX** (basic measures)
- **Data Visualization & Reporting**

---

## 📊 Dataset
The dataset contains transactional sales data including:
- Product information
- Sales quantity
- Revenue
- City/location
- Order dates  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- A small number of products generate a large share of revenue
- Certain cities consistently outperform others in sales
- Revenue varies month-to-month, indicating seasonal trends
